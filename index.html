Instructions

Game play: You play Pepe as the main character. The destination is to defeat a chicken boss at the end of the level. Along the way, you will encounter enemies that you can defeat by jumping on them or throwing a bottle of salsa at them. You can collect these bottles along the way on your journey. You can also collect coins along the way. Once you have collected all the coins, you will receive a bonus. Please note: As long as a bottle is still visible on the map, you cannot throw another one!
